# WHY SHOULD WE KEEP GOING OUTSIDE

We were recently having breakfast as a family when it suddenly crossed my mind how different it was in the 80s. I then asked my father about his childhood. "So why is it that people say that the 80s or 90s was the best time to be a child?" I asked. He then answered immediately with "because that was the last time kids were able to go outside". Followed by his story of a childhood play friend. We laughed at the fact that the kid was the only one that had Atari, and that he was asking other kids for money as a sort of rent-to-play. Lines of kids started lining up to get a chance to play. He clearly understood the concept of supply and demand as a young child.
